## Style Prompt

Runse product intro, styled after the Runse app icon: deep forest-teal canvas, hand-drawn cream glyph, with a mint pebble and a warm gold pebble. The product should feel calm, native, quietly crafted, and quietly powerful — selected text moving through a minimal transform workflow rather than a generic chat UI. Glassy panels are still allowed, but tinted into the icon's warm-cream / teal world rather than blue cyberpunk.

## Colors

- `#143228` - deep forest canvas (background base)
- `#1D4538` - mid forest (panels, scene fill)
- `#22513F` - lifted forest (interactive surfaces)
- `#EFE7D2` - cream primary text and primary mark (matches the icon glyph)
- `#B9C5B8` - sage secondary copy
- `#A8CDB5` - mint pebble accent (privacy / success)
- `#D2A85F` - gold pebble accent (highlights, warmth)
- `#7FB39A` - soft jade highlight (refractive)

## Typography

- Display: `Georgia`, used for large editorial product statements (the icon is a hand-drawn curled glyph; a serif keeps the same warm crafted register).
- Interface/body: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`, used because the visual reference is native macOS.
- Data labels: `"SF Mono", "IBM Plex Mono", ui-monospace, monospace` with tabular numbers.

## Motion

- Fluid wipe transitions that reveal the next scene through rounded, blurred cream/jade bands.
- Entrances should feel buoyant and material: small vertical floats, scale catches, soft blur settling into focus.
- Ambient pebble shapes (mint and gold) drift slowly behind the content with finite repeats; no infinite animation loops.

## What NOT to Do

- No neon cyberpunk grid or blue-purple gradient wash.
- No generic SaaS card grid.
- No fake chat app framing.
- No hard jump cuts between scenes.
- No plain black or pure white — use the deep forest and the cream from the icon.
